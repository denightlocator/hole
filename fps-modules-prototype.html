What each module would do

CloudsHide vanilla clouds. big win in sky-heavy scenes
WeatherStop rain/snow rendering and its sound. cheap
Entity DistanceShrink the entity render distance to a % of vanilla. most of the gain
Hide ShadowsDisable entity shadow blobs. cheap
Hide GrassSkip tall-grass/flower block rendering. helps in fields
ParticlesThrottle or drop particles. big win in fights
Entity CullingSkip drawing entities behind you / out of frustum. moderate
Particle LimitHard cap on simultaneous particles. cheap
Render DistanceForce a chunk distance regardless of vanilla setting. can hurt more than help
Everything above is client-side only and reversible — nothing is sent to the server, so no anticheat exposure. The real wins are clouds, entity distance and particles; the rest is a few frames each.
